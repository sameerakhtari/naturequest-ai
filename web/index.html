A NOTE FROM NATUREQUEST

Look with wonder.
Leave with care.

The AI can describe what it sees, but it can be wrong. Don't use it to decide whether plants, mushrooms, or animals are safe to touch or eat.

✎ The photos are analyzed by your own Ollama server, never a hosted inference service. Your saved journal stays in this browser.

A DIFFERENT KIND OF TECHNOLOGY

More outside.
Less online.

NatureQuest runs open-weight Gemma through Ollama on a machine you control. Your photos aren't shipped to a company for inference. No accounts, tracking scripts, GPS permissions, or cloud AI bills. When the model isn't available, handpicked missions and your local journal still work.
